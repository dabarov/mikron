Mikron

See the spacing behind any page.

Hold ⇧ Shift and drag over anything. Mikron redraws that part of the page as a live blueprint with its spacing, sizes and corner radii, and copies them as a spec.

Mikron is switched off right now. Turn it on from the toolbar icon to try it here.

  1. 1

    Hold Shift

    Move the mouse a little. The cursor turns into a crosshair and a hint follows it.

    ⇧ Shift
  2. 2

    Drag over anything

    Press and drag across the area you care about. Sizes, padding, gaps and radii appear as you go.

    ⇧ Shift+drag
  3. 3

    Release, then Copy

    Let go to pin the selection. Click Copy to take the numbers with you. Esc dismisses it.

    CopyorEsc
